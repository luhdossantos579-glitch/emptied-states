About The Emptied States

The Emptied States tells the story of America's abandoned malls, ghost towns, resorts and company towns from opening day to today, with dated photos and maps.

Who makes this

Vision Media publishes The Emptied States, a YouTube history channel. Our research and scripts are original. The narration uses a text-to-speech voice, a fact stated in every video description.

How we work

Every date and number comes from a source we name: newspaper archives, census tables, Sanborn maps, historical societies. The list for each video is in its description, and for articles, at the bottom of the page. Photos are public domain, openly licensed or our own, credited video by video on the credits page.
